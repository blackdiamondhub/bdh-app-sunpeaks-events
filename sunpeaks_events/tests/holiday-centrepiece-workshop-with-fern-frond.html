Bring a little festive beauty to your home with a handmade holiday centrepiece! Join floral designer Chelsea Hogan of Fern & Frond for a relaxed, hands-on workshop creating a seasonal arrangement to enjoy throughout the holidays.

Using a thoughtfully selected combination of greenery and seasonal decorative elements, you'll learn how to create a centrepiece with lasting appeal. Your arrangement will include a vessel and materials to create a beautiful piece for your holiday table, hearth or kitchen island.

Whether you're looking to add a special touch to your own seasonal décor or simply enjoy an afternoon of creativity, this workshop is a lovely opportunity to make something beautiful to take home.

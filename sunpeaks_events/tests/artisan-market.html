Shop Local Handmade Goods at Sun Peaks

We are delighted to expand our Market Day to host an Artisan Market again this winter.

Stroll through the Sun Peaks Centre and browse products from local vendors, featuring fine and functional work such as textiles, paintings, jewelry, candles, holiday and home décor, seafood, sweet treats and more!  

Don’t miss this opportunity to shop for one-of-a-kind gifts for your loved ones. Leave mall shopping and mass-produced items behind and step into a world of creative wonder and delight this holiday season! 

Shoppers are encouraged to bring their own reusable shopping bags to the market.

Details

FREE ADMISSION
Dates: Friday, December 11 to Sunday, December 13 
Location: The Sun Peaks Centre is located at the top of the village, across from the Kookaburra Lodge, beside the skating rink. 
Parking: Day visitor parking is available on Village Place or P2. Limited accessible parking is available onsite.

Washrooms are available onsite. 
Sorry, no pets or ski boots allowed.

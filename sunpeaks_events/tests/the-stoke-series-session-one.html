The Stoke Series - Session One

We invite skiers and riders of all styles to celebrate local park culture in this open-format rail jam series.

Rack up points throughout the series to be in the running for the ultimate grand prize at the season finale, The Garden Party. 

At the end of each session, the skiers and riders with the most points will receive a special prize for their performance, with additional prizes up for grabs for all participants.

DETAILS

Rules and Regulations

  • Entrants must be able to ride the Base Camp Terrain Park with confidence.
  • Helmets are mandatory.
  • Participants under age 19 must have a parent or guardian complete a release of liability on their behalf at the time of registration. No exceptions will be made.
  • We reserve the right to remove participants from the event if they fail to meet these requirements.
  • All times provided to participants are approximate and subject to change.
  • Event registration does not include a lift ticket.

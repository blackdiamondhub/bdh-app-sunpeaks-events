About Bill Fell

Bill Fell is a fine art black and white photographer who specializes in traditional film and print darkroom processing.

Bill began his career in Ontario where he designed and implemented photography and darkroom courses for both high school and college level students. As well he was a photographic resource guide for White Squall of Georgian Bay and for many years conducted photographic / kayak tours as part of Fell Photography.

Since moving to Sun Peaks, BC in 2013 he has conducted numerous photographic workshops for Sun Peaks Resort, TNRD and Art Zone Sun Peaks as well as participating in multiple group and solo art exhibitions.
